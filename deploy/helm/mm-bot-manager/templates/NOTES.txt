The mm-bot-manager Pod runs without a Service or Ingress.

Check its status:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }},app.kubernetes.io/component=application

View logs:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }},app.kubernetes.io/component=application -c mm-bot-manager

Readiness checks database reachability, not Mattermost WebSocket connectivity.
Complete the acceptance checklist in docs/kubernetes.md after installation.
